## Normalized geometric margin

The crucial measurement is a **normalized geometric margin**, not a raw linear score margin:

\[
\gamma_i=\frac{y_i\,(w^\top x_i+b)}{\|w\|}
\]

Raw linear margins can be made arbitrarily large by scaling \(w\). They cannot reuse the prototype cosine threshold without this normalization. Pass requires:

- every training and probe point correctly signed
- \(\min_i \gamma_i \ge 0.01\)
- the same perturbation gate as WRITEGEOM/ELIGMAP (`σ=0.01`, `n=20`, `≥19/20`)

D0 keeps cosine ranking as the control (expected crumb failure). D0 does not convert cosine crumbs into a pass by rescaling.
